Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 7, 2026

SportApp is built with privacy as a core principle. We believe that your fitness data belongs to you.

"SportApp does not collect any personal data and only sends activity data to Strava and HealthConnect upon user's explicit action."

Data Handling

All data processed by SportApp is handled locally on your device. This includes:

  • GPS Location data
  • Heart rate and other sensor data
  • Workout history and statistics
  • User profiles and settings

We do not operate any backend servers to collect, store, or analyze your personal information or fitness activities.

Third-Party Services

Strava

If you choose to connect your Strava account, SportApp will upload your activities to Strava only when you explicitly trigger a sync or have enabled the auto-export feature in settings. The data transmitted includes activity type, duration, distance, heart rate, and GPS path. This data is then subject to Strava's Privacy Policy.

Health Connect

SportApp integrates with Android Health Connect to read and write fitness data. This allows you to centralize your health information. SportApp only accesses this data with your explicit permission. Use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions Policy.

Permissions

The app requests several permissions to function (Location, Sensors, Notifications). These are used solely to provide the tracking functionality while the app is in use. We do not sell or share any data derived from these permissions with third parties (except Strava/HealthConnect as described above).

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
